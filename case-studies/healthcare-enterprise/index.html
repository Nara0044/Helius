01 — Challenge

Spiraling costs with no visibility

A large healthcare enterprise operating across multiple regions had watched its cloud bill climb steeply for three consecutive years. Despite significant investment in AWS and Azure infrastructure to support patient data platforms, analytics workloads, and digital health services, the engineering team had no clear picture of where the money was actually going.

Kubernetes clusters - the backbone of their microservices architecture - were running at just 30-40% utilization, yet were being billed at 100% capacity. Idle namespaces from deprecated services lingered unnoticed, accumulating costs month after month. Reserved instance coverage was poorly managed, leaving expensive on-demand spend in place where it could have been optimized away.

The team lacked dedicated FinOps expertise. Engineers were focused on reliability and feature delivery, not cost governance. Without visibility, accountability, or automation, cloud waste had become a structural problem - not an operational one.

02 — Solution

AI-FinOps from day one - no new hires

Encore AI's AI-FinOps Assistant connected to their AWS and Azure environments within hours - no dedicated FinOps hire required. It immediately began building a unified cost topology across 4,500+ instances, integrating directly with their CI/CD pipeline to map every dollar of spend to a specific team, service, and environment.

Autonomous AI Agents
AI agents continuously scan for waste, rightsize workloads, and reclaim idle resources - 24/7, without human intervention.
Unified Cost Visibility
A single pane of glass across AWS and Azure - broken down by team, namespace, service, and environment.
FinOps Workflows
Automated FinOps workflows handled reserved instance recommendations, rightsizing approvals, and namespace cleanup - with full audit trails.
Human-in-the-Loop Controls
For sensitive workloads, every automated action was reviewable and reversible with full approval workflows.

Beyond the automation, Encore AI surfaced cost attribution data directly to engineering teams - making every engineer cost-aware without turning them into FinOps specialists. Cost culture became embedded in the daily workflow, not bolted on as an afterthought.
